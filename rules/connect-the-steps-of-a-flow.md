---
title: Connect the steps of a flow
summary: Join the steps of a multi-step flow with a line so they read as one path with a clear order and next step.
category: navigation
principle: Uniform connectedness
confidence: established
sources: [ig-Del7XTgsuwI]
---

**Do:** Draw the steps of a checkout, onboarding, or setup flow as markers
joined by one line, with completed, current, and upcoming steps styled
differently.

**Don't:** Show the steps as loose, unconnected circles or labels. They read
as separate things with no order.

**Why:** Elements joined by a line read as one group, and the line shows the
order to read them in, from start to end.

**Spot it:**

- In code: a multi-step flow (`step`, `currentStep`, wizard, onboarding pager)
  whose step indicator renders separate dots or circles with no connecting
  line, or has no indicator at all.
- On screen: step markers float apart with no line showing their order.

**Notes:** ig-Del7XTgsuwI (1:01) shows four loose circles read as 4 groups
and connected circles as 1 group.
