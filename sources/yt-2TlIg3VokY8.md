An 11-minute video presenting six psychology principles, each as a before
and after pair of the channel's own mockups, plus a famous study and a brand
example. It tests nothing, so every number on screen is an illustration.
Several studies are misstated: the jam study is used for defaults, a
"70–90% never change defaults" figure has no source, and free samples
"raise sales up to 2,000%". It also contradicts itself on ethics. The
description warns against faking progress, but the narration praises a fake
head start, recommends a guilt-tripping decline label, and shows a
misleading price comparison. Several rules below keep the principle and
reverse the example.

**Skipped:**

- **Jam study at 0:45.** Already covered and disputed in
  [show-fewer-options-up-front](../rules/show-fewer-options-up-front.md).
  Here it is misapplied to defaults.
- **"Decision fatigue."** A label for a mechanism, not a rule, and it rests on
  ego depletion, which has replicated poorly.
- **A search button showing "12 results waiting."** A mockup detail with no
  support.
- **Restaurant decoy steak and estate-agent anecdotes.** Untested folklore.
- **Sponsor segments.**
