A 97-second reel, the fourth part of a 24-law series. It covers six
perception and decision laws. For each one it shows a broken and a fixed
version of a dark-theme mobile screen side by side, with a number for each:
percentage visible, contrast ratio, looks, keystrokes, groups, or conversion.
Most of those numbers come from the mockups themselves. The exception is the
jam study, which the reel cites as research.
